Biography

Hello, my name is Alexander Pritchard, and I am a designer! I have a primary focus on UI design, and I also enjoy making motion graphics!
I have experience in a multitude of programs, such as Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, and Premiere. I also have experience using 3d modelling software such as Maya, ZBrush, and 3DSMax.
My goal is to become a designer of motion graphics and artistic UI elements, as I have a great love for clean, vibrant, and smooth animated visuals!
